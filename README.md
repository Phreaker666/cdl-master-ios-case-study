# CDL Master — Published iOS Training App | Case Study

**Type:** Product case study only; no proprietary training manuals or employer diagrams included.

### Challenge
Commercial driver qualification and safety training require consistent, understandable procedural instruction and practical reinforcement.

### What I built
I designed and published an iOS training app for commercial driver instruction and trainer support. The app has been reported as used by more than **500 trainees/users**.

### Why it matters for GRC and technical operations
- Translate documented standards and complex procedures into usable workflows.
- Reinforce training and readiness checks.
- Communicate safety-critical details in accessible formats.
- Manage a product from requirements to deployment and iteration.

### Related experience
Seven years leading training, safety, and regulatory compliance in a transportation operation supporting approximately 400 drivers; trained approximately 100 drivers personally.

### Responsible disclosure
This case study is high-level. **No employer-owned technical diagrams, protected training materials, internal documents, driver identities, or private data** are published. Commercial transportation compliance is not being presented as direct SOC 2 or ISO 27001 audit experience.
